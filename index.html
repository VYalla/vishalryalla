Vishal R. Yalla

Aerospace & Mechanical Engineering ‘29 @ California Institute of Technology (Caltech)

Broadly speaking, my work is at the intersection of aerial intelligence and space robotics, where my current research is focused on generalizing aerial autonomy.

I’m a researcher @ Center for Autonomous Systems and Technologies (CAST) at Caltech under the Mory Gharib Research Group, where my research is centered on Symbolic Robotics & Reasoning Algorithms for aerial vehicles. My current work also extends to Visual & Semantic Robotic Perception, Navigation, and Control, as applied to aerial flight.

Currently I’m an intern @ NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) working on GNC (Guidance, Navigation & Control) and autonomy stacks for Skyfall Mars Helicopter. Previously, was working on the Science Data System & Operations for NISAR, JPL’s flagship Earth Science mission. I also work on methods for early mission formulation & concept design for JPL’s spaceflight engineering directive.

Prev, was at the Keck Center for Space Studies (KISS), Caltech’s & JPL think-and-do tank, and the Space Structures Lab @ Caltech. Before this, was at the University of Washington (UW) as a researcher at the Control & Trustworthy Robotics Lab (CTRL) / Autonomous Flight Systems Lab (AFSL), Autonomous Controls Laboratory, and Husky Satellite Lab, among others. In the space sciences, was a researcher at LIGO (Laser Interferometer Gravitational-Wave Observatory), the Green Bank Observatory, and CERN Elementary Particle Physics Experiment (EPE) Group, where my research was in multi-messenger astrophysics.
